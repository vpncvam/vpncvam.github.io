I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at [IIT Bhilai](https://www.iitbhilai.ac.in), where I work on the application of lattices in cryptology under the guidance of my incredible supervisor, [Dr. Dhiman Saha](https://dhimans.in), Associate Professor in the department. I am currently a Senior Research Fellow on **PIPETA**, a post-quantum IP encryptor and TLS accelerator funded by DST under the National Quantum Mission, where I study fault-injection attacks on post-quantum hardware.

Before this I worked on lightweight searchable encryption for financial databases, and on mitigating authentication relay attacks by false base stations in 5G at IIT Delhi. I hold an M.Sc. in Mathematics & Scientific Computing from MNNIT Allahabad.
