About

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Bhilai, where I work on the application of lattices in cryptology. My research is being carried out under the guidance of my incredible supervisor, Dr. Dhiman Saha, Associate Professor in the department. Presently, my work focuses on fault-injection attacks on lattice-based cryptographic primitives. I am also working on searchable symmetric encryption schemes that support range queries. I come from a mathematics background, having completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics and Scientific Computing at MNNIT Allahabad in 2018.
